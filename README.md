# Stranger Things

> "Friends don't lie."  
> A thrilling **sci-fi horror** series set in the 1980s where a boy disappears, uncovering government secrets and supernatural forces.

---

## Why I Love It

- **Nostalgic 80s vibe**
- *Strong character development*
- Suspense + mystery + friendship = ❤️

---

## Main Characters

| Character | Role | Why They're Great |
|------------|------|-------------------|
| Eleven | Girl with psychic powers | Brave, mysterious, and kind |
| Mike Wheeler | Loyal friend | Always stands up for his friends |
| Joyce Byers | Determined mother | Never gives up on her son |
